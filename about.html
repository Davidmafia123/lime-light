About Lime Light

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Lime Light began with a simple idea: to bring reliability and elegance to the essential systems of a home. We saw a world where homeowners had to choose between a skilled plumber and a stylish lighting store, but never found both in one place. We set out to change that. Starting from a small workshop, we've grown into a trusted name, known for our commitment to quality craftsmanship and our eye for brilliant design.

Our Mission

Our mission is to brighten homes and fix flows with unparalleled professionalism and care. We aim to be the single, trusted partner for our customers' most vital home needs, delivering peace of mind through expert plumbing services and inspiring living spaces with our curated lighting collections. We are dedicated to transparent pricing, timely service, and lasting solutions.

Why Trust Lime Light?

Certified Experts

Our team consists of licensed plumbers and knowledgeable lighting specialists who are passionate about their craft.

Premium Quality

We use only the highest-quality materials and source lighting products from reputable, design-forward brands.

Customer First

Your satisfaction is our priority. We offer upfront pricing, clear communication, and a guarantee on all our work.
